## Design Case
This portfolio documents my individual and group engagement or activities in Social Robot Design course at University of Twente (2026). My background in Computer Science and Engineering helps me dive into trying to find a solution the minute I see a problem. But this course made me realise the importance of the process of understanding and shaping the design tool. Each session in the course taught me a new aspect of robot designing. The design case I worked on across the course concerns the deployment of a MiRo-E social robot in student study spaces to support well-being. My understanding of that case shifted from session to session as it began as a software integration question and, by the embodiment and expressiveness sessions, a question about physical presence and social signal design came into picture. The group activities helped us brainstorm and work on the design tool we pictured. The different sessions are put under different headings in the index.
