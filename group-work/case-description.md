# Case Description

## Problem
Students (18–24) struggle with:
- Stress
- Overworking
- Lack of breaks
- Poor well-being

---

## Proposed Solution
A MiRo-E social robot that:
- Checks student well-being
- Suggests breaks
- Guides relaxation exercises

---

## Research Question
How can the MiRo robot support students (18–24) during study sessions to improve well-being?

---

## Literature (Examples)

- MiRo social interaction research
- Stress mitigation using robots
- Multimodal human-robot interaction

Backed up by literature in our domain and for our robot (MIRO-E).
