# Storybuilding

## Method
We used an Office-style scripted improv approach.

## Scenario 1
Robot interrupts student during study session.

Goal:
- Check well-being
- Balance helpful vs intrusive behavior

## Scenario 2 (Edge Case)
Deaf student interaction.

Goal:
- Explore communication failure
- Identify accessibility issues

## Insight
This method helped us explore emotional reactions and interaction dynamics.

## Experiments

- Theatre exercises with students
- Testing different question styles
- Observing facial expressions
- Comparing interaction styles

## Goal
Understand:
- Comfort level
- Intrusiveness
- Effectiveness of robot intervention
